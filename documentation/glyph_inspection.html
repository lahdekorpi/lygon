Typographic Anatomy & Design Observations

A detailed breakdown of key glyphs across the typeface, documenting their design characteristics, modifications made in Lygon, and points for visual assessment.

Glyph Orig Letter Category Design Characteristics & Observations
A A A Benchmark Asymmetric left cutout with ~95u to 110u gap. Serves as the golden ratio anchor for all other cutout reductions.
B B B Normalized Cutout reduced from 218u to 105u. Preserves full circular outer bowl curves without distortion.
K K K Normalized Cutout reduced to ~90u. Inner and outer diagonal junctions shifted equally, ensuring constant 182u stroke width with zero wedges.
P P P Normalized Bowl termination cutout reduced from 205u to 100u, bringing it into harmonic balance with A and B.
R R R Normalized Diagonal leg junction moved closer to stem (~100u gap); entire leg retains constant 182u stroke thickness from top to bottom.
j j j Refined Descender apex and tip extended leftward by 80 units based on user vector edit (j_modified.svg), providing a balanced round sweep.
k k k Redesigned Replaces original looped arch with modern straight diagonal arms matching capital K. Original loop preserved in stylistic set ss02.
g g g Harmonized Two-story open loop. Added point (797, 20) to Bold master, fixing the variable font weight drop across intermediate instances.
i i i Harmonized Harmonized contour hierarchy across Light, Regular, and Bold masters, eliminating the inverted exclamation mark bug in variable interpolation.
t t t Original Asymmetrical crossbar at x-height with gentle upward sweep at baseline. Consistent across family.
y y y Original Straight diagonal descender terminating with an angled cut at -380u below baseline. Clean and legible.
4 4 4 Original Distinctive open top-left vertex. Clean geometric numerals with open counters.
Q Q Q Original Straight horizontal-diagonal tail cutting outward from the lower right of circular counter.
€ € € Redesigned Crossbars now cut all the way through the back curve to the left, eliminating visual confusion with mathematical set membership symbols.
