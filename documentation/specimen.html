Geometric Quirks Fixed: Before & After

Side-by-side analysis of key letters where cutouts were halved to match letter A, uniform stroke widths were enforced, and lowercase j received a full swept curve.

Letter A (Proportion Benchmark)

Proportion Anchor
A Lygon Benchmark
A Original Sansation

The letter A served as the visual benchmark. Its natural internal empty space is approximately 95 to 110 font units. All letter cutouts were brought into harmony with this reference.

Letter B (Cutout Halved)

Gap: 105u vs 218u
B Lygon Refined
B Original Sansation

The original cutout between the stem and middle junction was 218 units wide, leaving excessive empty space. Lygon brings the junction closer (105 units) while preserving the natural outer bowl curvature.

Letter K (Uniform Arm Thickness)

Zero Wedges
K Lygon Refined
K Original Sansation

The inner apex was translated toward the stem while simultaneously translating the outer junction by the exact same distance. This ensures both diagonal arms maintain an exact, uniform 182-unit stroke thickness without any wedge skew.

Letter P (Bowl Cutout Harmonized)

Gap: 100u vs 205u
P Lygon Refined
P Original Sansation

The lower bowl stroke previously terminated 205 units away from the vertical stem. Lygon shifts this closure point closer (100 units) along its curvature, matching the proportion of letter A.

Letter R (Uniform Diagonal Leg)

Zero Wedges
R Lygon Refined
R Original Sansation

The diagonal leg in Sansation formed a severe wedge when inner points alone were moved. Lygon moves both the inner leg apex and outer bowl intersection together, guaranteeing a constant 244-unit stripe from top to bottom.

Letter k (Straight-Arm Redesign)

Modern Straight Arms
k Lygon Refined
k Original Sansation

Replaces Sansation's arched loop with a clean, contemporary straight-arm construction matching capital K. Diagonal arms span from x-height (1050) to baseline (0), with a normalized ~90-unit cutout gap and uniform stroke thickness.

Letter j (User Curve Refinement)

Round Sweep Refined
j Lygon Refined
j Original Sansation

Preserves Sansation's authentic round curve while extending the bottom apex and terminal tip leftward by 80 units. This smoothly continues the arc, resolving the overly straight, abrupt truncation of the original tail.

Euro Symbol (€ Crossbars)

Crosses Through Spine
€ Lygon Refined
€ Original Sansation

In Sansation, the two horizontal crossbars ended inside the inner counter, causing visual confusion with mathematical set membership symbols (like element-of). Lygon extends the crossbars all the way through the back curve, emerging cleanly on the left in accordance with standard typography.

Question Mark (? Toned Down)

Proportions Refined
? Lygon Refined
? Original Sansation

In Sansation, the rounded head of the question mark bulged excessively to the right, creating a distended mushroom-like shape and awkward spacing in sentences. Lygon tones down the rightward overhang by ~80 units, achieving a balanced, modern curve while preserving its geometric character.

Registered Symbol (® Inner R)

Matches Refined R
® Lygon Refined
® Original Sansation

In Sansation, the R inside the registered trademark symbol preserved the old, gaping cutout and wedge-shaped diagonal leg. Lygon synchronizes the inner R with the refined capital R geometry, featuring a harmonized cutout gap and uniform diagonal leg thickness.
