FONTLOG: Lygon Font Family

This file provides detailed information on the Lygon font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Lygon fonts
and any derivative works.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Lygon is a modern geometric variable sans-serif font family. It is a 
refined modernization of Sansation, originally designed by Bernd Montag (2011).

ChangeLog
---------
2026-09-23 Toni Lahdekorpi (https://github.com/lahdekorpi/lygon)
- Renamed font family to Lygon to comply with SIL OFL Reserved Font Name terms.
- Normalized cutout proportions on uppercase B, K, P, and R by halving the 
  internal gap (~90 to 115 units) to match the proportion anchor of letter A.
- Enforced uniform diagonal leg thickness on K and R, eliminating wedge distortion.
- Redesigned lowercase k with clean contemporary straight arms matching capital K; 
  preserved original looped arch under OpenType stylistic set 2 (ss02).
- Extended lowercase j with a smooth, authentic round sweep descender.
- Redesigned Euro currency symbol (€) so both horizontal crossbars slice cleanly 
  through the back spine curve to the left.
- Toned down the distended, ballooning round head of the question mark (?) 
  and inverted question mark (¿) by ~80 units.
- Synchronized the inner R of the registered trademark symbol (®) with Lygon's 
  refined capital R geometry.
- Removed forced fused ligatures (fi, fl, ff, ffi, ffl, fj, ffj) from OpenType 
  standard ligatures (liga) to ensure crisp, independent letter rendering.
- Engineered continuous variable font axes (wght 300 to 700) for upright and italic.
- Harmonized master topologies and point indices across all six master styles.
- Modernized OS/2 (Version 4, USE_TYPO_METRICS), STAT, hhea, post, and gasp tables.
- Generated 10 static instances across TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats.
